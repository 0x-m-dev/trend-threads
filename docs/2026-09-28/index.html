Footguns with Postgres "at time zone 'UTC'"

AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' on a timestamptz silently turns it into a timestamp without a zone. Month math then follows the session timezone, and timestamp vs timestamptz equality is always false. The ugly fix is to run AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' twice.
