I don't like passkeys

Hawksley says passkeys make personal security worse: lose the device and you can lock yourself out, Apple and Google become the recovery path, hardware keys still don't cover every login, and the backup story is easy to get wrong.

OpenJev

OpenJev is now SemIf, an independent browser-only experiment (not TypeSafe). Load an open model on WebGPU and either read typed option logits or have it write the same distribution as JSON. MiniCPM5 2B is the desktop default at 1.56 GB; weights stay in cache.

Bend 2 and the Vibe-Coding Trap

Liam Powell: Bend 2 wants humans to write laws and AI to write proofs the compiler checks. The homepage demo is 58 lines of laws and 442 lines of proof, and Bend never says formal verification. He vibe-coded the same demo in SPARK to show the field already exists.

Jemalloc 5.4.0

jemalloc 5.4.0 (Sep 17): 160+ commits of cleanup plus a pinned-extent flag for HugeTLB-style reuse. Tcache fill is now demand-based; seven old knobs are gone and silently ignored. free preserves errno, free_sized accepts NULL (C23), deadlock and TSD bugs fixed.

Read →

An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents

Fan et al. ran 176 matched harness settings on four models (SWE-Bench Verified, Terminal-Bench 2.1). Context management mostly stops overflow; rule-based elision then LLM summary wins. Planning props up weak models and saves money on strong ones. Bash-fluent models are cheaper without extra tools.
