Shipping JPEG XL in Chrome

Chrome 155 finally ships JPEG XL decode. 30-50% smaller than JPEG, HDR, lossless transcoding of old JPEGs, decoder rewritten in Rust (jxl-rs) because image parsers are a browser attack surface. They even needed a Rust SIMD feature so it wouldn't be slow. AVIF still exists; they say try both.

A font recreated from photographs of classic Commodore 64 keycaps

Someone photographed actual C64 keycaps and rebuilt the lettering as a font (TTF/OTF/woff2). Caps, numbers, £, f-keys, Hungarian accents, and all 63 PETSCII front legends. Lowercase types as uppercase because that's what was printed on the plastic. Unofficial, v1.107.
